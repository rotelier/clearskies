Scenario ideas

  • The storm: Fri 2 Oct lunchtime. Does she spot the warning, pick a fix and turn on heavy-week mode?
  • The window: Thu 15 Oct 5.40pm, then cancel her class. Does she use time she didn’t plan for?
  • She adds it herself: ask her to add a new course deadline in the app (+ Add) on a busy day.
  • No cover: fill the partner’s evenings, then open heavy week. What is left when nobody can take the kids?
  • Both sides: set something up on one phone, then switch to the other and see it land.
  • Ignore the storm: let her skip the fixes, jump a week, and see how the week looks.
  • Heavy week by choice: on a week the app hasn’t flagged, ask her to turn on heavy week and write her own message to sambal.
  • Her own time: book something for her, then open You. The hours and the aim line move together, and nothing is earned for crossing it.
  • The check-in: Sun 25 Oct 8pm, send the check-in.
